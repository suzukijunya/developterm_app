Changes made for this app: each FBX was converted to glTF (binary, geometry and
skeleton only), textures were resized to 1024/512 px JPEG (hair and glasses
opacity textures to 1024 px WebP with alpha), and a small mask.png was made per
avatar (clothing / hair regions) so the app can recolour clothes and hair.
